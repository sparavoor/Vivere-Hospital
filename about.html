Our Story

Pioneering Holistic Healthcare Since 2010

Founded by Dr. Priya Sharma, Vivere Wellness Hospital emerged from a vision to bridge the gap between ancient healing traditions and modern medical science. Our journey began with a simple belief: true healing addresses the whole person, not just symptoms.

2010 - Foundation

Established with a vision to integrate traditional healing wisdom with modern medical practices, creating a unique healthcare approach.
